About

About Our Place Restaurant

Our Place is a locals' favourite in the Platzl — Kimberley's pedestrian downtown — where everyone meets for good times, good service, good prices and especially good food. They specialize in homemade traditional fare: Eggs Benjamin, the Special Loaded Omelette, 'Our Fries' and the Platzl Burger, and are open daily until 2 PM for breakfast and lunch.
